# Cookie Policy Addendum — Analytics Consent

VATranscribe uses a category-based consent model.

## Necessary storage

Necessary cookies and browser storage may be used without optional consent when required for:

- authentication;
- CSRF protection;
- refresh-token security;
- security audit evidence;
- language and interface preferences.

## Analytics storage

Analytics storage is disabled by default. It may be enabled only after the user grants analytics consent.

Supported analytics integrations are environment-driven and may include Yandex Metrika or GA4. Tracking identifiers must not be hardcoded in the repository.

## Marketing storage

Marketing pixels and advertising attribution are disabled by default. They require explicit marketing consent and a separate legal/processor review before activation.

## Consent version

Current consent version: `2026-06-10`.

When this version changes, users must be asked to confirm their preferences again.
